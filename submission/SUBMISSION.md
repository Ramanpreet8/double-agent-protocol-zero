## Project title
Double Agent: Protocol Zero

## Project description (paste this)
A real-time spy game for 4 to 8 players on their own phones. Friends join with a 4-letter room
code, no accounts or installs. Each mission, anonymous intelligence reports arrive, and some are
forged by hidden Double Agents. Compare the reports for contradictions, spend your one private
Verify on a suspicious fact (then decide whether to tell the truth), and vote to approve or
reject every report. Rubber stamps reveal which reports were genuine. After up to three missions
the team names its suspects. Rounds take about 10 minutes. The server decides every role, vote,
score and winner and never sends secrets to the wrong player. It also handles refreshes,
reconnects, disconnects, duplicate taps and late joiners.
